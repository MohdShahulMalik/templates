FAQ

Questions, answered

Who is Natgenic for?

Natgenic is made for people noticing increased hair fall, gradual thinning or a hereditary pattern of hair loss, and who want a dedicated oil as part of their regular hair-care routine.

Can I use Natgenic if I have genetic hair loss?

Yes — Natgenic is formulated with hereditary thinning in mind. It is a hair-care product, not a medical treatment, so it is intended to support your hair-care routine rather than replace medical advice. If your hair loss is sudden, patchy or accompanied by scalp irritation, please consult a dermatologist.

How often should I use it?

Follow the usage instructions on the bottle. In general, applying it regularly and massaging it into the scalp as part of a fixed weekly routine matters far more than using a large amount occasionally.

How long should I use Natgenic before evaluating results?

We suggest a minimum of 60 days of consistent use. Hair changes slowly, so take photographs on day 1 and compare them on day 60 in the same light and from the same angle. Results differ from person to person.
